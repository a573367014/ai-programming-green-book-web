题型: 填空题 难度: 中等
题干: 两个向量[1, 2, 3]和[2, 4, 6]的余弦相似度是____。 (复习题)
参考答案: 1
解析: 向量[2, 4, 6] = 2 × [1, 2, 3]，两个向量方向完全相同，夹角为0°，cos(0°)=1。余弦相似度只关心
方向不关心大小，所以即使长度不同，方向相同就是1。

题型: 阅读理解题 难度: 困难
阅读材料: “This distributional hypothesis—that words that occur in similar contexts tend to
have similar meanings—is the fundamental assumption underlying all modern word
embedding methods. The idea was first formulated by linguist John R. Firth in 1957: ‘You
shall know a word by the company it keeps.’ […] Word2Vec operationalizes this hypothesis by
training a neural network to predict either a word from its surrounding context (CBOW) or the
context from a word (Skip-gram). The resulting word vectors have been shown to encode rich
semantic and syntactic information, even though the training signal is purely based on co-
occurrence statistics.” （来源：John R. Firth, 1957年, 语言学理论; 以及Mikolov et al., 2013年,
Word2Vec论文, Introduction章节） (延展题)
题型: 单选题 难度: 困难 题号: 1
题干: According to the passage, what is the “distributional hypothesis” that underlies all
modern word embedding methods?
选项A: Words that have similar spellings tend to have similar meanings 选项B: Words that occur
in similar contexts tend to have similar meanings 选项C: All words are distributed equally
across all contexts 选项D: Word meanings are distributed across multiple languages
正确答案: B
解析: The passage directly states the distributional hypothesis: “words that occur in similar
contexts tend to have similar meanings.” This is operationalized by the CBOW and Skip-gram
architectures in Word2Vec, which train on co-occurrence patterns—the same “relationship
network” that Saussure described philosophically nearly a century earlier.
题型: 简答题 难度: 困难 题号: 2
题干: The passage notes that Firth’s 1957 insight—“You shall know a word by the company it
keeps”—and Saussure’s structuralist linguistics (described in this section) arrive at similar
conclusions about meaning. In 1-2 sentences, explain how the training signal in Word2Vec
(predicting a word from its context) is a computational implementation of this idea. (延展题)

predict “fish” when the context is “cats eat ___” or to predict “cat” when surrounded by
“the ___ eats fish”, the model is forced to encode exactly the “company” (context) that
