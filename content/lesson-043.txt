训练。

题型: 单选题 难度: 困难 题号: 1
题干: According to the passage, why is a relatively small number of demonstrations (10,000-
50,000) sufficient for instruction following?
选项A: Because the pre-trained model already learns best from small datasets 选项B: Because
the pre-trained model already possesses much of the needed knowledge—it just needs to
learn how to use it in response to instructions 选项C: Because larger datasets cause
catastrophic forgetting 选项D: Because human demonstrations are inherently of higher quality
than pre-training data
正确答案: B
解析: The passage explicitly states that “the pre-trained model already possesses much of the
knowledge it needs—it just needs to learn how to use it in response to instructions.” This
aligns with the chapter’s comparison: pre-training builds a knowledgeable generalist; fine-
tuning teaches it how to apply that knowledge to specific tasks.
题型: 简答题 难度: 困难 题号: 2
题干: The authors describe the process as starting with a “pre-trained language model” and
fine-tuning it to “follow instructions.” Connect this to Section 2-7’s claim that “监督微调不
是重新学习语言，而是在已有能力基础上调整行为模式.” In 1-2 sentences, explain why 10,000-
50,000 demonstrations are enough while pre-training needed 300 billion tokens. (延展题)
参考答案: ● Pre-training’s 300 billion tokens build the model’s fundamental language
understanding, world knowledge, and reasoning ability from scratch. Fine-tuning’s 10,000-
50,000 demonstrations don’t teach new knowledge—they teach a new interaction pattern
(question → helpful answer). Since the model already understands language, it only needs to
learn when to produce answers versus when to continue text, hence the much smaller data
requirement. This is exactly why the chapter says “微调不是重新学习语言，而是在已有能力基础
上调整行为模式.”
